Caesar Salad

Description

Caesar salad is a timeless classic featuring crisp romaine lettuce hearts tossed in a rich, creamy, and tangy garlic-anchovy dressing. Crowned with crunchy golden croutons and shaved Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese, every forkful offers an irresistible balance of texture and savory depth.

This salad makes a delightful standalone light meal or the perfect appetizer to accompany hearty pasta and grilled meats. Making the dressing from scratch elevates it far beyond any bottled alternative.

Ingredients

  • 2 large heads of romaine lettuce, washed, dried, and torn into bite-sized pieces
  • 1 cup artisan bread, cut into 1/2-inch cubes
  • 3 tablespoons extra virgin olive oil
  • 1/2 cup shaved or grated Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese
  • 2 cloves garlic, finely minced
  • 2 anchovy fillets (or 1 teaspoon anchovy paste)
  • 1 large egg yolk
  • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard
  • 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lemon juice
  • 1/3 cup mild olive oil or vegetable oil
  • Salt and freshly cracked black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C). Toss cubed bread with 2 tablespoons olive oil, minced garlic, a pinch of salt, and pepper on a baking sheet.
  2. Bake bread cubes for 10 to 12 minutes, tossing halfway through, until golden and crunchy. Set aside to cool.
  3. In a medium bowl, mash anchovy fillets and minced garlic together with the back of a fork to form a paste.
  4. Whisk in the egg yolk, Dijon mustard, and lemon juice until well blended and slightly frothy.
  5. Slowly drizzle in 1/3 cup oil in a thin, steady stream while whisking vigorously to create an emulsion. Season dressing with salt and cracked black pepper.
  6. Place washed and thoroughly dried romaine lettuce in a large salad bowl.
  7. Drizzle dressing over the greens and toss gently until all leaves are evenly coated.
  8. Add the homemade croutons and shaved Parmigiano-Reggiano cheese, give one final gentle toss, and serve immediately.
