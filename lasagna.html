Lasagna

Description

This classic homemade lasagna features rich, savory layers of hearty meat sauce, creamy ricotta filling, tender pasta sheets, and melted mozzarella cheese. Baked to golden perfection, it offers an irresistible aroma and satisfying comfort in every single bite.

Whether you are hosting a festive family dinner or prepping meals for the week ahead, this traditional Italian-American favorite is guaranteed to impress. It pairs wonderfully with a crisp green salad and warm garlic bread.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 lb lean ground beef
  • 1 lb sweet Italian sausage
  • 1 medium onion, diced
  • 4 cloves garlic, minced
  • 2 cans (28 oz each) crushed tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons tomato paste
  • 2 tablespoons chopped fresh basil
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese
  • 1 large egg
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 4 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Bring a large pot of lightly salted water to a boil. Cook lasagna noodles according to package directions until al dente. Drain, rinse with cold water, and lay flat on parchment paper.
  2. In a large Dutch oven or skillet over medium heat, cook the ground beef, Italian sausage, and diced onion until meat is browned. Add minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, fresh basil, Italian seasoning, salt, and pepper. Simmer uncovered on low heat for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  4. In a medium mixing bowl, combine ricotta cheese, egg, grated Parmesan, and a pinch of black pepper until smooth.
  5. Preheat your oven to 375°F (190°C).
  6. Spread 1 cup of meat sauce over the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish. Arrange 4 lasagna noodles lengthwise over the sauce.
  7. Spread one-third of the ricotta mixture over the noodles, top with a layer of meat sauce, and sprinkle with 1 cup of mozzarella cheese. Repeat layers twice more, finishing with meat sauce and the remaining mozzarella on top.
  8. Cover loosely with aluminum foil (sprayed with cooking spray to prevent cheese from sticking) and bake for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake uncovered for an additional 20 minutes until bubbly and golden brown.
  9. Let the lasagna rest for 15 minutes before slicing and serving.
